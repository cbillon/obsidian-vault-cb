Get an inside look at the Moodle Marketplace — a new platform designed to better support plugin developers, and improve how Moodle users discover, evaluate, and access plugins and integrations. In this session, you’ll learn what the Marketplace is, why we’re building it, and what problems it’s designed to solve for plugin providers and customers alike.

We’ll explore key insights from our user research, highlight what we’re prioritising for the initial launch, and share where we are in the development process. You’ll get a clear picture of the value the Marketplace will deliver at launch and a glimpse of our future roadmap.

Whether you’re a developer, service provider, educator, or site admin, this talk will show how the Marketplace is evolving to meet your needs — and how you can get involved and help shape its future.
